Davidson & Company LLP

Davidson helps people navigate complexity with confidence through trusted accounting, audit, tax, and advisory services. As a Brand Designer, I help shape how our brand comes to life across digital and physical experiences.

My work focuses on creating clear, user-focused experiences that strengthen our brand, connect with our audiences, and make every interaction feel intuitive and engaging.

Vancouver Police Museum & Archives

Developed with the Vancouver Police Museum, Adaptive Mode transforms static exhibits into a guided, accessible experience for visitors of all abilities. I led the UX process from initial research through high-fidelity prototyping, delivering a modular design system built on WCAG and POUR principles.

Jazzdor Festival

Jazzdor is a contemporary jazz festival known for its bold visual identity and expressive spirit. This concept project explored how an experimental, form-driven brand system could be translated into an intuitive and accessible digital experience.

Working as part of a five-person team, I focused on UX research, information architecture, and interaction design. My work centered on creating a clear user experience that balanced visual expression with usability, helping users easily discover artists, venues, schedules, and tickets.
